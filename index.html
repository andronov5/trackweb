04 / MEET DAY, SORTED

LESS SCRAMBLE.
MORE GAME FACE.

Pack the night before. Know the plan. Save your energy for the starting line.

Before you leave ＋

Confirm the meet location, arrival time, transportation, and your race or event with your coach. Check the forecast and pack layers.

Check the official schedule ↗
At the meet ＋

Find the team area. For track & field, check your heat, lane, flight, or relay leg. For cross country, check the course and starting box. Follow your coach’s warm-up plan and cheer for teammates.

After you compete ＋

Cool down, hydrate, and check in with a coach. Support athletes still competing, clean up the team area, and confirm the plan before leaving.

05 / YOUR STARTING LINE

NEW HERE?
YOU’RE IN
GOOD COMPANY.

First season or fifth, there’s room to grow. Bring the effort. We’ll bring the team.

Ask about joining ↗
  1. 01

    Get cleared to compete.

    Check eligibility, athletic registration, and current physical requirements through Northfield Athletics.

  2. 02

    Meet your coaches.

    Ask about fall cross country, spring track & field, the next practice, and what to bring. You don’t have to have it all figured out.

  3. 03

    Show up ready.

    Bring running shoes, water, and layers. Confirm the schedule, arrive on time, and be ready to learn.

  4. 04

    Be a Nighthawk.

    Keep up with schoolwork, communicate about absences or injuries, respect officials, and back your teammates in every season.
