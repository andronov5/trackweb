MEET DAY, SORTED

LESS SCRAMBLE.
MORE GAME FACE.

Pack the night before. Know the plan. Save your energy for the starting line.

Before you leave ＋

Confirm the meet location, arrival time, transportation, and your race or event with your coach. Check the forecast and pack layers.

Check the official schedule ↗
At the meet ＋

Find the team area. For track & field, check your heat, lane, flight, or relay leg. For cross country, check the course and starting box. Follow your coach’s warm-up plan and cheer for teammates.

After you compete ＋

Cool down, hydrate, and check in with a coach. Support athletes still competing, clean up the team area, and confirm the plan before leaving.
