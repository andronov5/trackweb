Ask about joining ↗
  1. 01

    Get cleared to compete.

    Check eligibility, athletic registration, and current physical requirements through Northfield Athletics.

  2. 02

    Meet your coaches.

    Ask about fall cross country, spring track & field, the next practice, and what to bring. You don’t have to have it all figured out.

  3. 03

    Show up ready.

    Bring running shoes, water, and layers. Confirm the schedule, arrive on time, and be ready to learn.

  4. 04

    Be a Nighthawk.

    Keep up with schoolwork, communicate about absences or injuries, respect officials, and back your teammates in every season.
